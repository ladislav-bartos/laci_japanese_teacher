## 文法 — W3D4: 〜ところ／〜たところ／〜どころではない／〜どころか — Mark 4/5, Reorder 0/2

One clean, fixable miss in the mark section: これから電車に乗ったところです mixes a future-oriented
これから with past-tense 乗った — the two contradict each other. It should be これから...乗るところ
です (about to board), since 〜たところ is reserved for something that just finished, not something
about to happen. Both reorder items broke apart a fixed chunk that needed to stay together
(しようと思っていた in #6, あって directly after the given clause in #7).

## Weak points (Anki candidates)

1. **Repeated-wrong-answer reuse in Kanji Part A** — two days running now (Day 3: しゅへい reused
   for 期限/経済; Day 4: めんせい reused for 面積/服装, ちりゅう／ちりょう reused for 燃料/資料). Worth
   flagging as a habit to watch for mid-drill, not just a content gap.
2. **〜たところ vs 〜るところ** — tense-matching rule: これから／ちょうど今から pairs with 〜るところ
   (about to); ちょうど今／たった今 pairs with 〜たところ (just finished). Today's miss mixed これから
   with the past-tense form.
3. あきる（飽きる, get tired of repetition）vs あきれる（be amazed/appalled at behavior）; ちかう
   （誓う, swear an oath）vs いのる（祈る, pray）.
4. みっともない（shameful/undignified behavior）vs 恋しい（miss/long for, a feeling word — different
   part of the sentence entirely）; くやしい（frustrating, standard near-miss reaction）vs あこがれる
   （admire/aspire to be like, unrelated word type）.
5. 埋めた（うめた, bury）— read as とめた（止めた/stop), keeping the correct て-form ending but
   substituting an unrelated verb stem, same shape as Day 2's misses.
6. Listening: today's misses both hinged on a reveal arriving *after* an earlier plausible-sounding
   surface detail — practice holding the first answer loosely until the dialogue actually resolves.
