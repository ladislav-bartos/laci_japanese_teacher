## 文法 — W3D3: とたん／あげく／末／かと思ったら — Mark 1/5, Reorder 0/2

The clearest, most fixable pattern of the day: a mirror-flip on とたん(に). One sentence used とたん before
a command (「食べたとたんに、飲んでください」) and was marked ○ (should be ×); another used とたん correctly
for an immediate unexpected result (「使ったとたん、かゆくなった」) and was marked × (should be ○). とたん
needs a completed, non-volitional past-tense trigger and result — never a command. Also missed: an あげく
sentence with a valid negative-result structure marked wrong, and 行く vs 行った in another あげく sentence
(あげく always attaches to Vた).

## Weak points (Anki candidates)

1. **とたん(に)** — needs Vた + an unexpected, non-volitional past-tense result; never pairs with a
   command/request. Today's #1/#2 flipped this in both directions — highest-priority grammar card.
2. **あげく(に)** — attaches to Vた (not Vる: 行くあげく→行ったあげく), and typically closes with a negative
   outcome after enduring something (待たされたあげく、まずい料理を食べさせられた is the textbook shape).
3. 交流（こうりゅう, exchange/interaction with people） vs 交換（こうかん, exchange of objects）; 担任
   （たんにん, the teacher in charge） vs 責任（せきにん, responsibility, abstract）.
4. Physical-action verb pairs: はう（crawl）vs すべる（slide/skate）; すれちがう（pass by）vs ぶつかる
   （collide）; のぞく（peek）vs にらむ（glare）; はがす（peel off）vs 閉じる（close）; 曲げる（bend）vs
   ひざをつく（kneel).
5. Today's 14 kanji (商,個,相,交,効,限,全,共,忘,経,過,責,任,負) — Part A near-zero again; #6/#10 both
   answered しゅへい despite being different words (期限／経済), worth double-checking for slips like this
   during the drill itself.
6. Reading/listening: today's misses centered on tracking reversals or absolute claims within a passage
   rather than the surface gist (筆者の主張 の "no reason at all" vs "some reason"; the retracted
   appointment time in 聴解 #3) — practice re-reading/re-listening for the *final* stated outcome, not
   the first plausible-sounding one.
