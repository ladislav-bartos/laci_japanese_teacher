**Notes on the misses:**
- **未満 (#5)**, not 以下 — 以下 ("or less") *includes* the boundary number; 未満 ("less than")
  *excludes* it. The sentence says "18歳は含まれない" (18 itself is not included), which is exactly
  what 未満 means — 以下 would wrongly include 18-year-olds.
- **満ちている (#8)**, not 老いている — 危険に満ちている is a fixed phrase meaning "full of danger."
  老いる means "to age/grow old," which doesn't fit "danger" semantically at all — this one's worth
  a flashcard just for the fixed collocation 危険に満ちる.
