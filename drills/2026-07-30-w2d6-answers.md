## 文法 — Reorder — 0/2

| # | Target | Your order | Correct order | ✅/❌ |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 6 | 学生とともに教師も成長していくべきだ | 2→1→4→3 | 4→1→2→3 | ❌ |
| 7 | 係員の指示にしたがって車を止めてください | 1→4→3→2 | 2→3→1→4 | ❌ |

**#6** — your order (2→1→4→3) reads 成長／教師も／とともに／していく, which puts とともに in the middle
of the phrase with nothing appropriate on either side. とともに has to attach directly onto 学生 (the
thing being paired with — 学生**とともに**), giving 学生**とともに**（4）教師も（1）成長（2）していく
（3）べきだ ("along with the students, the teachers too should keep growing"). The fix is to find what
とともに is attaching to (学生) and place it immediately after.

**#7** — your order (1→4→3→2) reads 車を／止めて／したがって／に指示 — this leaves したがって floating
with no target to attach to. にしたがって has to attach directly onto 指示に (指示に**したがって** =
"in accordance with the instructions"), and the whole phrase then modifies the final verb: 指示に（2）
したがって（3）車を（1）止めて（4）ください ("please stop your car in accordance with the staff's
instructions"). Same underlying issue as #6 — find the noun the pattern attaches to first, then build
the rest of the sentence around it.

Both reorder misses share one root cause: **にしたがって／とともに attach directly onto the noun right
before them** (指示に＋したがって, 学生＋とともに); once that pairing is fixed in place, the remaining
pieces have only one sensible order left.
