## 文法 — W3D1: とおり／まま／ことに／あまり — Choice: 3/5, Reorder: 1/2

### Choice

| # | Question | Your answer | Correct | ✅/❌ |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 予定（とおり／どおり）行われた | a | b | ❌ |
| 2 | （不思議な／不思議）ことに | a | a | ✅ |
| 3 | 興奮（あまり／のあまり） | b | b | ✅ |
| 4 | 思う（まま／どおり）自由に | b | a | ❌ |
| 5 | （くやしい／くやしさの）ことに | a | a | ✅ |

**Notes on the misses:**
- **#1** — correct is **b** (予定どおり), not とおり. When どおり attaches directly onto a noun with
  no の in between (compound-word style: 予定どおり, 約束どおり), it voices to どおり; とおり is used
  after の (予定のとおり) or after a verb (言ったとおり).
- **#4** — correct is **a** (思うまま), not どおり. 〜まま(に) here means "following one's own
  feelings/impulse freely" (思うままに自由にかいた絵 = a picture drawn freely, however one felt like);
  どおり implies matching an external standard (a plan, an instruction), which doesn't fit "自由に."

### Reorder

day-of-the-week shape. Reading and listening were the weakest live-comprehension sections today (0/2

long; what's being checked *when* he says 確認すれば) rather than the general gist — worth flagging

the topic" skill.
